# Corridor/Tunnel B0 Known Limitations

- This qualifies one corridor member only: two parallel walls with no ceiling,
  enclosure, doorway, clutter or dynamic obstacle.
- The exact eight-row bounded command schedule is wiring/identity evidence, not
  a performance, safety, clearance or policy-quality test.
- The separate plane is validated as USD topology; no same-scan plane-hit claim
  is made for the wall-only selected Warp Mesh.
- Runtime hit counts are specific to the frozen baseline, exact seed and
  fixture. They are evidence, not thresholds or portable expectations.
- Only B0 is executable here. B1-B8, real-robot scenarios, training, scale and
  M8 remain unauthorized.
- The frozen runtime emits an existing deprecation warning for
  `set_external_force_and_torque` on stdout; all formal stderr streams are
  empty and no runtime/package change was made.
